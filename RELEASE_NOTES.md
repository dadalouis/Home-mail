### Beta: fewer errors and nicer notifications
- A device that is offline (for example a wall tablet without local push) no longer fills the Home Assistant log with errors, and no longer holds up notifications to your other devices.
- The text in a notification no longer shows links or messy leftovers, and is cut off after about 140 characters.

This is a beta. In HACS, turn on **Show beta versions** for Home-mail to get it. Restart Home Assistant after updating.
